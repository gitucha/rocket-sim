The physics under the hood

Each step (dt = 0.01 s) the sim sums three forces on the rocket and integrates:

F = T − D − m·g(h), with a = F / m

• Thrust T is constant while fuel remains. Fuel burns at ṁ = T / (Isp·g₀), so mass drops and acceleration climbs during the burn.
• Drag D = ½·ρ(h)·v²·Cd·A, always opposing motion. Air density uses an exponential atmosphere ρ = 1.225·e^(−h/8500).
• Gravity weakens with altitude: g = g₀·(R/(R+h))², R = 6371 km.
• Dynamic pressure q = ½ρv². Max Q is the point of highest aerodynamic stress, a key design load.
• The ideal (no drag, no gravity) Δv is the Tsiolkovsky equation: Δv = Isp·g₀·ln(m₀/m_f).
